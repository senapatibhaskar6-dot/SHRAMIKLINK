# ShramikLink - Founder & Platform Instructions (Bhaskar Senapati)

This project is **ShramikLink**, an indigenous B2B SaaS platform for industrial manufacturing, tea gardens, and regional enterprises in Assam and Northeast India, founded by **Bhaskar Senapati**.

### Core Architecture & Business Logic:
1. **Four Key Stakeholder Roles**:
   - Industry HR & Principal Employer Management
   - Industry & Garden Supervisors
   - Labor Contractors / Sardars (Gang Leaders)
   - Industrial Workers & Plantation Shramiks
2. **Key Compliance Safeguards**:
   - **Compliance-Locked Billing**: No vendor/contractor billing invoice can be generated without verified EPF/ESI statutory deposits.
   - **Dual Sector Governance**:
     - *General Manufacturing*: Factories Act, CLRA Form XVI/XVII, Payment of Bonus Act, 1965 (8.33% - 20%).
     - *Tea Garden / Plantation*: Plantations Labour Act, Task/Hazira green-leaf plucking metrics, Sardar gang logs replacing traditional *"hajira bahi"*, and tripartite pre-Puja bonus agreements.
   - **Revenue Model**: ₹1 per worker per day high-volume micro-SaaS model.
